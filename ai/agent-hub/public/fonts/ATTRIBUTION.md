# Font sources

`host-grotesk.woff2` is [Host Grotesk](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Host+Grotesk) and `atkinson-hyperlegible-mono.woff2` is [Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Atkinson+Hyperlegible+Mono). Both are the Latin subsets of the variable fonts served by Google Fonts, distributed under the [SIL Open Font License 1.1](https://openfontlicense.org). They are bundled because the service's Content-Security-Policy allows fonts from this origin only.
